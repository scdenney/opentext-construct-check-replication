# Reading the results

The check estimates one thing: the difference between treatment arms in the
share of answers that mention a construct. It shows whether respondents
articulate the consideration the design assumes. That is necessary for a
mechanism and does not establish one.

## The target

- **Recovered.** The estimate is significant (randomization p < .05) and has
  the expected sign. Say that treated respondents mentioned the consideration
  more (or less) often, by how many percentage points, from what share in the
  control arm.
- **Expected sign, not significant.** Say so, and report the interval. In an
  evaluation of 205 primary contrasts from 138 experiments, about nine in ten
  estimates had the expected sign and about two in three were significant. A
  result of this kind is weak evidence, not evidence against the design.
- **Significant in the opposite direction.** This is rare (four of 205
  contrasts in the evaluation). Report it plainly. Check the contrast coding
  and the wording of the question before interpreting it.

Before reading a miss as evidence about the construct, look at two things.

1. **The size of the arms.** With fewer than about 200 answers in the smaller
   arm, the check has little power.
2. **The study's own closed-ended measure**, if there is one (the main outcome
   or a manipulation check). Estimate the same contrast on it. In the
   evaluation, most contrasts that the text did not recover were ones in which
   the closed-ended measure showed no difference either. If neither moves, the
   treatment did little in this sample, and the text is not the problem.

## The placebos

A placebo should not rise. If one rises, the treatment brought to mind
something the design did not intend, or the question for the target and the
question for the placebo overlap. Report it.

Placebos often fall. A short answer has room for one or two reasons, so a
treatment that makes one consideration prominent leaves less room for others.
A falling placebo is not a failed check.

## What the estimate does not show

- It is not a mediation effect. An effect on what respondents write does not
  show that this consideration carries the treatment's effect on the outcome.
- Answers can be rationalizations written after the choice.
- In online panels, some answers may be written by software. Check for
  duplicated or implausibly fluent answers on the discovery fold before the
  lock is written.

## Languages and models

The evaluation covers English well, and Korean, Chinese and German in the
author's own studies. For other languages the evidence is thin. In that case
check the model's labels against a small hand-coded sample from the discovery
fold, or run a second model from another family and compare.

A small model is adequate for yes/no questions. If the local copy of the model
is compressed (quantized), compare it once with the full-precision model on a
sample of discovery-fold answers.
